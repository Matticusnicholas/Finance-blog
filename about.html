About The Money Blog

Real talk about personal finance from someone who learned most of this the hard way.

Who Am I?

I'm just a regular person who spent way too long being bad with money before figuring things out. No finance degree. No professional credentials. Just years of mistakes, reading, learning, and eventually getting my financial life together.

I grew up in a household where money wasn't really discussed. We weren't poor, but we weren't wealthy either. Nobody taught me about investing, compound interest, or even basic budgeting. I went to college, got a decent job, and promptly spent everything I earned plus some.

Credit card debt, no emergency fund, no retirement savings at 27 – that was me. I thought retirement was something I'd "figure out later" and that investing was only for people who already had money.

What Changed

In my late twenties, I stumbled into the personal finance corner of the internet. Blogs, Reddit communities, podcasts. I started reading about people who were saving significant portions of their income, investing consistently, and building real wealth – not through luck or inheritance, but through boring, consistent habits.

The more I learned, the more I realized how much I'd missed. Simple concepts that could have saved me thousands of dollars if I'd learned them earlier.

So I started implementing what I learned. Paid off my credit cards. Built an emergency fund. Started investing. Opened the right accounts. Made plans. It wasn't quick or dramatic – just steady progress over years.

Now I'm in my mid-30s, debt-free except for my mortgage, with healthy retirement accounts and a clear path toward financial independence. Nothing flashy, but stable and secure.

Why This Blog

I write because I wish someone had written this for me 10 years ago.

Most financial advice is either:

  • Too basic ("save money, spend less than you earn!" wow thanks)
  • Too complicated (written for finance professionals)
  • Too preachy (judgment about lattes and avocado toast)
  • Written by people who have always had money and don't understand starting from zero

I try to write the way I'd explain things to a friend over drinks. Practical, honest, sometimes opinionated, hopefully helpful. I've made most of the mistakes I write about, which I think makes the advice more useful.

Disclaimer

I'm not a financial advisor, accountant, or lawyer. Nothing on this site is professional financial advice. It's just my personal opinions and experiences based on my own learning journey.

Everyone's situation is different. What worked for me might not work for you. Use what's helpful, ignore what isn't, and consult professionals for major decisions.

That said, I try to be accurate and helpful. If you spot errors or have questions, I genuinely want to know.

Let's Figure This Out Together

Personal finance doesn't have to be complicated. It doesn't require a finance degree or a six-figure income. It just takes some basic knowledge, decent habits, and time.

If you're starting from zero, or starting over, or just trying to get better – you're in the right place. We're all figuring this out as we go. Let's do it together.
